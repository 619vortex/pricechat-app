# News Desk redesign, 2026-11-23: one editorial decision pipeline

Owner's brief (2026-11-23): "Make the Terminal behave like a competent, fast, independent financial news editor ... Stop patching individual
symptoms. Fix the underlying decision-making." Owner's decisions the same day: immediate breaking only for verified MAJOR events; the four
market-pause fixes ("a liquidation-data problem must never silence independently verified market news"); a second price source for the
Nasdaq and the Dow, and no automatic move without reliable verification; quality above quantity. Diagnosis: `2026-11-23-diagnosis.md`.
Branch `owner/fix/nd-major-moves`. Nothing here is deployed.

## Root causes

1. **A chain of keyword gates, each able to refuse alone.** The newsworthy test had consequence rules per story type: money only counted
   for hacks and liquidations, a stock's % only when the story was filed as "market", sinkings and takeovers were in no list. Off-list meant
   "none", and "none" was a refusal: Nvidia +27% on its CEO's return, a $44B takeover, a $12M hack, a Russian warship sunk, a $2T rally.
2. **The same question asked twice with different rules.** Newsworthiness at release and again at delivery: 8 released events in a week were
   refused on both platforms, their slots used and nothing sent.
3. **Five repeat checks with different rules**, the cross-lane one treating a whole war as one event (a country name was a "specific" anchor).
4. **Diversity as a hard block with no time limit** (fixed and deployed: ce9e312).
5. **Silent and misleading rejections** (paused market moves recorded as "no slot before it went stale"; the skip alert's "no reason recorded").
6. **One market pause for everything**, tripped by one small token's real liquidation squeeze re-noted every 5 minutes, so the S&P, Nasdaq,
   Dow, gold and silver moves of 11-23 never went out.

## The pipeline (`app/newsdesk/importance.py`; `app/newsdesk/events.py` review / memory / _release_slot)

Every waiting candidate, every pass, in this order; each step records its outcome on the event (`facts.decisions`, newest 20; `ev.why`):

| step | what | kind |
|---|---|---|
| 1. holds | the owner's block; a pause (market_pause: every market post; liq_pause: liquidation posts only); a measured move no second market confirmed | hard |
| 2. **the** repeat check (`events.memory`) | released-event signature (knows material developments), what the channel delivered this week, the event across every lane (country names are not anchors; two different named targets are two events; a wave of strikes is 12 h) | hard |
| 3. thread rules | an update needs its original out; a second move on one asset within the cooldown is an update | hard |
| 4. **the judgement** (`importance.judge`) | did something happen (a stated figure, people hurt, a completed hard event)? how big (one scale for money, moves, casualties, events)? who does it touch? how sure (outlets, official, hedged, nothing done yet)? still news? never news: opinion, explainer, feature, live page, round-up, reaction, question | the one editorial call |
| 5. scheduling | **MAJOR verified (2+ outlets or official): at once**, outside the slots and the diversity rule (min gap 20 min, flood guard 6/24 h); near an unfilled slot it goes as that slot's post. **Everything else: the six slots**, best SIGNIFICANT first, else NOTABLE (two posts in a row from one desk hold a NOTABLE only). A MAJOR measured move between slots: the market lane | plan |
| 6. factual checks | unchanged (preflight; at delivery the claim, numbers, live price and second-source checks). A story failing them is rejected and the next one is tried | hard |
| 7. delivery | repeat + owner block; the final check uses the same judgement on content (no second rule); pictures: licensed photo or article preview/link, else held for review | hard |

Tiers: major >= 72 (needs 2 outlets or an official source), significant >= 54, notable >= 42, else noise. Every verdict prints its parts:
`major: a major company's share move of 27%, a leadership change (score 77; size +34, reach +5, sources +8)`. Trending uses the same judgement.

## Market data: the four pause fixes and the second source (`safety.py`, `sources.py`, `brand_manager/sources.py`, `livedata.py`)

1. **The breaker counts distinct problems** (kind, subject, UTC hour): one figure re-read every pass is one anomaly.
2. **The pause is scoped.** Liquidation anomalies set `liq_pause` (liquidation posts only); price anomalies (an implausible move, two markets
   disagreeing, mismatched figures) set `market_pause` as before. The owner's market_pause still holds every market post. Admin shows both,
   each with its own resume.
3. **A liquidation hour is judged against the same hour's market** (Hyperliquid's hourly candles, read only when the history rule would hold
   it): far above the coin's usual hour is believed when that hour's range (3x) or volume (4x) shows a squeeze; never when the total is more
   than 2x the hour's traded value on Hyperliquid; the absolute ceilings stay. Checked on real data: BTC's $100M hour on 11-23 13:00 UTC (range
   2.7x, volume 5.3x) is believed; the same $100M in a calm hour (0.9x, 0.6x) is held; the 11-21 $10.1B unit error is above the ceiling.
   PIEVERSE (not listed on Hyperliquid) stays held, and now pauses liquidation posts only.
4. **The second source compares with a tolerance that grows with the move** (+25% of the move: silver's 1.63% gap on a 6% day passes, a unit
   error does not). **Nasdaq Composite and Dow** now have one: Nasdaq.com's own quote (COMP; the Dow through DIA x100, 0.21% from the index on
   11-23). **A second market that can't be read is "not verified"**, and a measured move without a confirming second market is never
   published automatically (`facts.two_sources`), at release or at sending.

Production note: the current market_pause (set automatically on 11-22 by the liquidation squeeze) stays on after a deploy; resuming it is
the owner's switch in Admin. From then on, liquidation problems only pause liquidation posts.

## Known limits

- Pure-headline judgement: a strong story with a weak headline and one outlet can stay under notable until a second outlet confirms it.
- The five repeat detectors still exist underneath `events.memory` (one call, one reason); merging their algorithms is a later step.
- Data posts ("biggest one-day drop since ...") are judged as moves and wait for a slot unless the market lane takes them while major.
- Nasdaq.com may refuse cloud addresses; if it can't be read from production, Nasdaq/Dow moves are held as "not verified" (by design).
